# 02. Training data

Longlist of datasets for Guardian v2 (`urduguard`), a harm classifier for English, Roman Urdu, Urdu script and mixed text. Labels: `violence_threats`, `harassment`, `hate_group` (non-religious), `self_harm`, `sexual`, `benign`. Checked 2026-10-21 via the Hugging Face Hub API, the datasets-server `size` and `first-rows` endpoints, dataset cards and GitHub repos. Row counts are what the API returned on that day.

## Hard rules

1. Aegis 2.0 TEST split (`nvidia/Aegis-AI-Content-Safety-Dataset-2.0`, 1,964 rows) and every IndicGuard test split (`l3cube-pune/IndicGuard` `Urdu/test.json` and the other nine languages) are the evaluation set. Never train or tune on them. IndicGuard `Urdu/test.json` carries the 1,964 Aegis test rows under `tag=Generic` plus 1,000 jailbreak and 505 culture-adaptive rows. Card: https://huggingface.co/datasets/l3cube-pune/IndicGuard
2. Training data must be licensed for a product. OK: CC BY, CC BY-SA (share-alike noted), Apache, MIT, ODC-BY, CC0. EVAL-ONLY, never training: CC BY-NC (IndicSafe `ppattnay/IndicSafe`, XL-Sum `csebuetnlp/xlsum`), research-only, "contact authors", gated research-use agreements, unclear or missing licences.
3. Religious content is filtered out of every dataset by a script (keyword lists in Urdu script, Roman Urdu and English). Datasets with a religious-hate, blasphemy or religion category are flagged in the table and that category is dropped before the filter runs.
4. Nemotron Safety Guard (any version) is never used. It is trained on translated Aegis, including the test split.

## Label mapping used for the counts below

Aegis 2.0 / IndicGuard `violated_categories` to Guardian labels: `Violence`, `Threat`, `Guns and Illegal Weapons` to `violence_threats`; `Harassment`, `Profanity` to `harassment`; `Hate/Identity Hate` to `hate_group`; `Suicide and Self Harm` to `self_harm`; `Sexual`, `Sexual (minor)` to `sexual`; `prompt_label=safe` with no category to `benign`. Rows whose only categories are out of scope (`Criminal Planning/Confessions`, `Controlled/Regulated Substances`, `PII/Privacy`, `Illegal Activity`, `Fraud/Deception`, `Needs Caution`, etc.) are excluded, not relabelled benign. Counts on `Urdu/train.json`, `tag=Generic` (30,007 rows, same ids as Aegis train): violence_threats 3,354; harassment 2,608; hate_group 2,233; self_harm 445; sexual 1,538; benign 9,556; out of scope 9,362; no label 911. A rough Urdu religion-keyword pass hits 2,523 of the 30,007 rows across all categories; the real filter will remove some of every class, most from `hate_group`.

RUHSOLD Task 2: `Abusive/Offensive` + `Profane/Untargeted` to `harassment`; `Sexism` to `hate_group`; `Normal` to `benign`; `Religious Hate` dropped. RUT/PURUTT: `Toxic=1` to `harassment` (coarse, audit first); `Toxic=0` to `benign`.

## Training mix

Rows available per language form and Guardian label from TRAIN-verdict sources only, before the religion filter and before dedup. "Native" means written that way by a person. "Translit." means produced by transliterating the Urdu-script row of a parallel English/Urdu pair (Aegis train id = IndicGuard Generic id). Nothing is LLM-written.

Roman Urdu gap in numbers: native Roman Urdu has 0 rows for `violence_threats`, `self_harm` and `sexual`, and 603 rows for `hate_group`. Every Roman Urdu row in those four classes (about 7,570 before the religion filter: 3,354 + 445 + 1,538 + 2,233) is a transliteration of a machine-translated Urdu row of an English Aegis prompt. Native Roman Urdu is concentrated in `harassment` (15,288) and `benign` (63,525).

Synthetic generation plan, in order:

1. Transliterator: train a Urdu-script to Roman Urdu model on PURUTT (72,771 pairs, CC BY 4.0), Dakshina ur (CC BY-SA 4.0) and Roman-Urdu-Parl (Apache-2.0 on the mirror, 6.3M pairs). Apply it to the IndicGuard Generic train rows after the religion filter.
2. Spelling augmentation: sample word-level variants from the Dakshina lexicon attestation counts (several romanisations per word) and from a variant table mined from RUT (e.g. `hy`/`hai`/`he`, `nhi`/`nahi`/`nai`). Two to three variants per transliterated row.
3. Mixed rows: splice English spans from the Aegis source row into the transliterated row at clause boundaries, using the English/Urdu alignment the parallel ids give. No LLM-written content.
4. Hold out: nothing generated from any test id. The transliterator is also used on IndicGuard `Urdu/test.json` Generic rows to build a Roman Urdu eval slice, kept separate and never trained on.

## Decision

- TRAIN: Aegis 2.0 train + validation (en); IndicGuard `Urdu/train.json` + `Urdu/valid.json` with `tag=Generic` only (ur); RUHSOLD from the GitHub TSVs minus `Religious Hate` (roman_ur); RUT / PURUTT with `Toxic` mapped to `harassment` after a 200-row audit (roman_ur + transliteration pairs); Jigsaw Toxic Comment and Civil Comments after dropping religion-identity rows (en); HateXplain minus religion targets (en); Davidson via the MIT GitHub repo (en); `Adnan855570/Urdu_Hate_Speech` only after a 200-row audit (ur). Transliteration resources: PURUTT, Dakshina, Roman-Urdu-Parl.
- EVAL-ONLY: Aegis 2.0 test; IndicGuard all `test.json` files; IndicSafe (ur slice); FIRE 2021 Urdu abusive and threat sets (threat set is the native `violence_threats` eval); Urdu cyberbullying corpus; WildGuardTest; ToxicChat; BeaverTails; MHC Hindi; PolyGuardPrompts (hi); RTP-LX (hi); TRAC-1/2; Bohra 2018; HASOC Hindi; PRISM; OLID; XL-Sum ur (benign false-positive check).
- UNUSABLE: Nemotron-Safety-Guard-Dataset-v3 / CultureGuard data; PolygloToxicityPrompts; DAMO-MultiJail; LinguaSafe; Khan 2021 and Akhter 2020 (no data link); HASOC "Urdu" (does not exist as its own set); UCI Roman Urdu sentiment.
- Religion filter runs on every TRAIN source after category drops. Datasets with an explicit religion category: RUHSOLD, IndicGuard (Culture_adaptive), Civil Comments (identity columns), HateXplain (targets), MHC (targets), Aegis (inside Hate/Identity Hate, no column).
- Open items: confirm Roman-Urdu-Parl's original licence; download Jigsaw from Kaggle and count `threat`; audit 200 RUT `Toxic=1` rows to confirm the `harassment` mapping; open the Urdu cyberbullying xlsx for a row count; decide whether `Sexual (minor)` rows (1,074 in the full Urdu train file) are kept under `sexual` or dropped.
